# Introduction to Neural Networks

Neural networks are function approximators built from layers of weighted connections.

## Perceptrons

The perceptron is the simplest neural unit: a weighted sum passed through an activation function.

## Backpropagation

Backpropagation computes gradients of the loss with respect to every weight using the chain rule, enabling gradient descent training.

## Convolutional layers

Convolutional layers share weights across spatial positions, making them ideal for images and other grid-structured data.
